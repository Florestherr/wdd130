About Us

Welcome to Dry Oar Rafting! We are passionate about providing unforgettable white-water adventures on some of the most beautiful rivers in the world. Our commitment to safety, customer satisfaction, and environmental stewardship ensures an exceptional experience for every adventurer.

Our History

Founded in 1986 by a group of passionate river guides, Dry Oar Rafting started with a simple vision: to share the exhilaration and beauty of the river with others. From humble beginnings with just a few rafts and a lot of grit, we've grown into a premier outfitter, renowned for our expertly guided trips and deep respect for nature.

Over the decades, we've explored countless rapids, introduced thousands to the joy of rafting, and fostered a community that values adventure, education, and conservation. Our journey has been one of continuous learning, adapting to new challenges, and always striving to enhance the rafting experience. We're proud of our legacy and excited to continue creating lasting memories on the water.
